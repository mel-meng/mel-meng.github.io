Houston-Style ICM Data Flag Cheat Sheet

Use flags to show where model data came from — and where it is in the model-building workflow.

Source tags answer “where did this come from?”
Workflow tags answer “what did we do, and what may happen next?”

ASM · Assumed INF · Inferred LP · Low Point

Source tag: What information supports this value? Workflow tag: What happened to this item, and what may need to happen next? Key idea: Keep the model moving without losing track of unfinished work.
